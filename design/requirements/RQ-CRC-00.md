## Item fields

### Description

This is a qualitative laboratory-developed test that screens average-risk adults
aged 45 and older for colorectal cancer using a self-collected stool specimen.

The assay combines a methylated DNA marker panel with a fecal immunochemical measurement
of haemoglobin. The analysis pipeline is the software component: it consumes
instrument output, computes a composite classifier score, applies the validated positivity
threshold, and produces a qualitative result of POSITIVE, NEGATIVE, or INVALID for release
to the ordering clinician.

The test is performed exclusively within the performing reference laboratory, which is
certified under CLIA to perform high-complexity testing. It is not distributed as a kit and
is not performed at any other site.

### Intended use

Intended for use as a screening test in average-risk adults aged 45 and older. A POSITIVE
result indicates that diagnostic colonoscopy is warranted. A NEGATIVE result does not rule
out colorectal cancer or advanced adenoma, and does not remove the need for screening at
the recommended interval.

The test is not intended for diagnosis, for surveillance of patients with a personal
history of colorectal cancer or adenoma, or for individuals at high risk due to inherited
syndromes or inflammatory bowel disease.

### Regulatory context

Oversight of this test sits with CMS under the Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments
(CLIA), rather than with FDA premarket device review. The FDA laboratory-developed test
final rule was vacated in full on 2025-03-31, and that decision was not appealed.

Accordingly, the controlling obligation is establishment of analytical validity under
42 CFR 493.1253 before any patient result is released: accuracy, precision, analytical
sensitivity, analytical specificity, reportable range, and reference intervals. Records
supporting result release are maintained as electronic records under 21 CFR Part 11.
